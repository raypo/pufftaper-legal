# Privacy Policy — PuffTaper

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026
**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

PuffTaper ("the App") is a self-tracking and motivation tool that helps adults cut down and quit vaping or nicotine pouches through a gradual taper plan. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App.

The short version: **PuffTaper collects nothing.** The App has no user accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you log stays on your device.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device, and only there:

- Your plan settings (product type, baseline usage, nicotine strength, taper length, plan start date, container price)
- Your usage logs (one entry per logged use, with a timestamp)
- Your craving events (timestamp, and whether you resisted or slipped)
- A small "today" snapshot shared between the App and its home-screen widget through an on-device App Group container

This data never leaves your device except as part of your own device backups (for example, an iCloud or computer backup you have enabled in iOS settings). Backups are provided and controlled by Apple and by your own settings; we cannot access them.

## 2. Information we receive

None. Because the App has no accounts and no backend, we do not receive, collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data — including your logs, your settings, your name, your email address, or your location.

## 3. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your payment details. The App checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's signed transaction data; no purchase information is sent to us. Apple's handling of your payment and App Store account is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

## 4. Tracking, analytics, and advertising

- No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- No advertising of any kind
- No tracking across apps or websites, and no use of the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
- No data shared with third parties, because no data is collected

## 5. Health-related information

The entries you log are self-tracking wellness data that you create for your own use. They are stored only on your device as described above. PuffTaper is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice or treatment, and does not diagnose or treat nicotine dependence or any other condition.

## 6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

- Delete individual log entries at any time in the History tab
- Erase everything using **Settings → Reset** in the App
- Delete the App, which removes its locally stored data (copies inside your own device backups are governed by your backup settings)

## 7. Children

PuffTaper is intended for adults who legally use nicotine products and want to stop. It is not directed at children, and — as with all users — we collect no data from anyone.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal data held by a company. Because PuffTaper collects and holds no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete — your data is entirely under your control inside the App, as described in Section 6. If you have any questions, contact us at the address below.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect in a new App version.
